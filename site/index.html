About

Hi, I'm Chants.

I help small and growing businesses build a stronger online presence through creative, practical and strategic digital marketing. I enjoy finding the balance between creativity and strategy, creating work that not only looks good but has a clear purpose behind it.

With more than 15 years of experience in design and more than 8 years in social media, I've developed a broad range of skills across social media, graphic design and email marketing. I work closely with my clients to understand their business, their audience and what they want to achieve.
